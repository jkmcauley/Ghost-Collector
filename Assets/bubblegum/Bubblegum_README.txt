BUBBLEGUM
Big Typephoon
Digital font by Thomas W. Ziller

ABOUT THE TYPEFACE

Bubblegum is a bold, playful display typeface based on lettering used in
Skagway grocery store advertisements in Grand Island, Nebraska in the 1980s and 1990s. Digitized by
Thomas W. Ziller in the 1990s, it retains the lively character and irregular
shapes of the original lettering.

HISTORY

Bubblegum was first released as shareware in the 1990s.
The original font carries a 1994 copyright notice, and the surviving font file
contains a Fontographer record dated November 14, 1995. It later became widely
distributed on free-font websites and has appeared in commercial products,
craft projects, games, packaging, and other designs.

The original release included capital-style characters in both the uppercase
and lowercase keyboard positions, along with numerals and a limited selection
of punctuation and symbols. Some lowercase-key characters differed slightly
from their uppercase counterparts.
